# Audio Safety Rules

- AUDIO-001: Never overwrite the original recording.
- AUDIO-002: Never cut solely at an ASR timestamp.
- AUDIO-003: Preserve acoustic margins around consonant onsets and word endings until reviewed.
- AUDIO-004: Do not delete uncertain speech; request human review.
- AUDIO-005: Do not assume the last take is the best take.
- AUDIO-006: Inspect joins for clicks, clipped phonemes, and room-tone jumps.
- AUDIO-007: Preserve names, dates, numbers, amounts, legal citations, and source-script wording.
- AUDIO-008: Save a complete, versioned EDL for every render.
- AUDIO-009: Reopen and validate every rendered file before reporting success.
- AUDIO-010: Trace every automatic decision to ranges, evidence, confidence, and engine/model version.
- AUDIO-011: Keep source media local unless the user approves a specific external service and dataset.
